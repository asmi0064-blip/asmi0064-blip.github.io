Origin

A person sits at a café working on a laptop, an everyday scene that would have been almost unimaginable in 1977. The device in front of them holds more computing power than entire supercomputers of that era, compressing what once filled entire rooms into something portable and silent.

Social Isolation is a pressing issue. Remote work culture has dissolved the boundary between office and public life, turning places like this into temporary nodes of productivity rather than social interaction, where connection increasingly happens through screens rather than across tables.
